Wholesale Beverages from Europe: A Practical Buyer's Guide

Understanding bulk soft drink and energy drink sourcing, packaging formats, pallet orders and cross-border distribution.

Soft drinksEnergy drinksPallet & truckload supply

Buying beverages wholesale involves more than choosing a brand. Importers, retailers and distributors need to assess packaging, minimum quantities, freight arrangements and destination-market requirements. This guide outlines the essentials of sourcing European beverage products for commercial resale.

Popular wholesale beverage categories

Carbonated soft drinks

Cola, orange and lemon-lime drinks are commonly supplied in aluminium cans and PET bottles. Packaging size and case count affect storage and transport planning.

Energy drinks

Classic, zero-sugar and flavoured energy drinks can be relevant to convenience stores, fuel retailers and regional distributors. Confirm assortment and local labelling requirements.

Mixed wholesale enquiries

Buyers may wish to source several brands together. Ask suppliers whether mixed-brand orders are supported and how pallet minimums apply to each product.

Understanding pallet quantities and freight

Wholesale beverage orders are often calculated in cases, pallets or full truckloads. A standard EUR pallet measures 1200 × 800 mm, but cases per pallet vary with bottle or can size and stacking configuration. Confirm the precise unit count for each SKU before comparing quotations.

For international shipments, request clarity on delivery terms, freight costs, lead times, customs responsibilities where applicable, and required product documentation. Shipping feasibility and compliance depend on the destination country.

Checklist before placing a wholesale beverage order

  1. Verify the supplier's company and trade details.
  2. Confirm exact product variants, origin, case size and pallet configuration.
  3. Ask for a written quotation and agreed delivery terms.
  4. Check shelf life, packaging labels and applicable import requirements.
  5. Clarify payment, insurance, transit time and handling of damaged goods.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum quantity for wholesale beverages?

Minimums differ by supplier and product. Some suppliers, including ATMFMCG, publish a one-pallet minimum; confirm the exact requirements before ordering.

Can several beverage brands be ordered together?

Some suppliers allow combined enquiries across brands. The final order structure, pallet requirements and freight terms should be confirmed in the quotation.

Do EU-produced beverages require import documentation?

Requirements depend on the destination and shipping route. Ask the supplier and your customs or compliance adviser which documents, labels and permits are necessary.
